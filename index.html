Who I Am

I'm Mohamed Alaa Hussein — a Data Engineer studying Communications & Electronics Engineering at university, with a deep focus on the full data pipeline lifecycle.

My engineering background gives me something most data engineers lack: I think in systems. The same rigour I apply to signal processing — isolate the noise, amplify the signal — I apply to data: validate at the source, transform cleanly, deliver reliably.

I build ETL/ELT pipelines, star-schema data models, and data quality layers. My work is built to be reproducible, documented, and maintainable — not just "it works on my machine."
