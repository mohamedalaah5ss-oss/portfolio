About Me

Who I Am

CAIRO · EGYPT

I'm Mohamed Alaa Hussein, a Data Engineer focused on turning raw data into trusted, analytics-ready datasets. I build ETL/ELT pipelines, design clean data models (star schemas), and implement validation + structured logging to keep data reliable. My background in Communications & Electronics Engineering gives me a strong analytical foundation — I approach data systems the same way I approach signal processing: understand the noise, clean the signal, deliver value. I care about clarity, reliability, and measurable impact — not fragile scripts.
